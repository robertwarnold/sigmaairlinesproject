System: Aircraft Hydraulic System

Purpose:
Provide hydraulic pressure to operate aircraft actuators.

Components:
1. Hydraulic pump - generates hydraulic pressure.
2. Hydraulic reservoir - stores hydraulic fluid.
3. Hydraulic lines - transfer fluid to actuators.
4. Pressure sensor - monitors hydraulic pressure.
5. Actuator - converts hydraulic pressure into movement.

Operating conditions:
The system operates under varying loads and temperatures.

Requirements:
- Maintain sufficient hydraulic pressure.
- Detect abnormal pressure conditions.
- Prevent uncontrolled actuator movement.
- Minimize fluid leakage.
